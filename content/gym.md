The gym is located in the pool building, accessible from the outdoor podium area on L3. It features
a range of equipment:

## Treadmill

The gym has one Vision T600 treadmill, supporting a variety of built-in exercise
programmes.

Please turn the treadmill off at the wall when finished.

## Stationary Cycle

The stationary cycle also features several exercise programmes. It needs to be turned
off at the wall when you finish using it.

## Multi-gym

The gym is equipped with a Matrix G1-MG30 3-Stack Multi-Gym.

The multi-gym has three positions (each of which can be separately used at the same time),
each supporting two weight exercises.

### Chest and Shoulder Press

Adjust the seat position using the yellow knob. Weight can be adjusted, as can starting
height.

### Lat Pulldown and Low Row

This position should either be used with the seat extended back, using the lower handle
for a low row, or with the seat forward, pulling down on the upper handle.

Please don't ever use the lower handle with the seat forward to attempt a bicep curl, as
the equipment is not designed for this, and it can permanently damage the cable, requiring
expensive repairs to the machine.

### Leg Extension & Prone Leg Curl

Adjust the seat following the instructions on the sticker for each exercise.
