The pool is accessible from the outdoor podium area on L3 - enter via the gym.
Attached to the gym are toilet and shower/change facilities.

## Pool rules

The Special Rules of the Owners Corporation include the following rules on pool usage:

* children below the age of 12 years must be accompanied and
supervised by an adult at all times.
* hours of use are 6:00am to 10.00 p.m.
* no food, alcoholic or other beverages, smoking, glass containers or receptacles of any kind are permitted in the area.
* only swimwear attire is allowed, topless and nude bathing prohibited.
* restrict use to residents only, not permit visitors and guests to use the facilities.
* diving, running, horseplay and the like are strictly prohibited in the pool areas.
