Title: Arrange to move in/out/heavy items
---
Per the Owners Corporation rules, if you are moving anything larger than a single person can
carry without causing property damage (for example, while moving in or out of the building,
or having furniture delivered), you need to book in with the Building Manager.

To organise a move, please complete the form linked below. It is also an opportunity to subscribe to our residents email / urgent SMS list, used to provide important information to
residents about what's happening in the building.

# Bookings
Must be arranged at least 2 days prior to the intended move. **Times will be subject to lift availability. It is
recommended that bookings are made as early as possible in order to avoid disappointment.**

Lifts are not to be used unless the **LIFT COVERS** are in place. **Bookings** are to be arranged by
completing the form link below, which will be recorded and sent to the Building Manager.

If you fail to make an appointment and someone else is using the lift, access will be denied to your carrier.

# Access

Carrier access is only available via Queens Lane. **Do not under any circumstances prop open doors.** You need to
inform your carrier that due to height restrictions, high vehicles cannot access the car park.

# Times

You'll only be able to book on weekdays (excluding public holidays) when no one else has booked. Two slots are
available each day:

* 8:30 AM - 12 PM
* 12 PM - 3:30 PM.

You must ensure your move starts and finishes within the booked slot.

# Contact

Should you need to contact the Building Manager, please call 0407 881 020 between 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM weekdays.
Please book your move using the link below before contacting the Building Manager.

# Rules applying to moves

1. No resident will be permitted to move into the building unless first making a booking through the link below.
1. The lift, entry foyers, all floor corridors including carpets and walls must be left in a clean condition after moving is complete. The resident will be responsible for the cleanliness of common areas, damage to lift walls, corridors, carpet, doors etc. **An account will be rendered for any repairs or additional cleaning that may be required**.
1. Care must be taken to ensure that fire sprinkler heads are not struck by any object whatsoever. **A resulting fire alarm and callout charge will be assessed to the party responsible.**
1. The resident must ensure that all cartons and packing crates are removed from the premises. Dumping of CARTONS or unwanted furniture is not permitted **in any part of the premises (including garbage chutes)**.
1. Use only the designated lift fitted with protective covers and follow the instructions given by the Building Manager.


# Link to the form

[Go to the form](https://citycondos.civicrm.org/civicrm/movesearch). Remember to verify your information and
click 'Register' on the second page, or your booking will not be saved. To subscribe to the resident's mailing
list, you'll also need to confirm your email address by clicking the link on the email you receive (if you are
not already on the mailing list).
