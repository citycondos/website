Title: Arrange to move in/out/heavy items
---
Per the Owners Corporation rules, if you are moving anything larger than a single person can
carry without causing property damage (for example, while moving in or out of the building,
or having furniture delivered), you need to book in with the Building Manager. The Building
Manager will arrange access through the back gate, opening of doors, and importantly, will
reserve a lift for your move and hang protective covers on the lift to prevent it from being
damaged.

The Building Manager works only on weekdays (excluding public holidays) from 8 AM to 4 PM.
To allow time for lift protection to be installed and removed, you move must start after 8:30 AM
and be completed by 3:30 PM. Unfortunately, moving large items is not permitted outside this
time.

To organise a move, please complete the below form. It is also an opportunity to subscribe to or
unsubscribe from our residents email / urgent SMS list, used to provide important information to
residents about what's happening in the building.
