The tennis court is part of the outdoor podium area on level 3. It needs to be booked by recording your name and lot number
against the hour block on the booking sheet on the corkboard in the gym. To ensure everyone gets a chance to use the court, it can only be booked for an hour at a time (i.e. booking two consecutive hour blocks is prohibited).

The tennis court is only suitable for tennis - playing other ball sports on it is prohibited.

Please be mindful that noise can carry from the podium area to neighbouring apartments, so should be kept to a minimum.

## Rules

The special rules of the Owners Corporation require the following in relation to the tennis court:

* children under the age of 12 years must be accompanied by an adult.
* hours of use are 8 a.m. to dusk.
* guests and visitors must be accompanied by an Occupier at all times and limited to four (4) per unit.
* no food, smoking, glass, or breakable objects permitted within this area.
* bookings are required at all times to be registered in the bookings book located in the gym and are to be limited to seven (7) days in advance, no block bookings allowed, all bookings must contain unit number as well as resident name, bookings accepted for a maximum period of one (1) hour.
* no other games other than tennis are to be played on the tennis.
* appropriate white-soled tennis footwear must be worn at all times.
