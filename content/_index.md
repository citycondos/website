---
Title: City Condos
---
City Condos is a 23 storey residential apartment building located at 416 St Kilda Road, Melbourne, VIC 3004.
It has 159 apartments (most with undercover parking) and features facilities including a gym, pool, BBQ area,
tennis court, front garden, guest lobby and two recently modernised lifts.


The website is maintained by the Owners Corporation for City Condos (formally, Owners Corporation PS416159S) to
provide information to current and prospective residents and owners.


Lots are privately owned, and so to buy or rent in the building, you'll need to deal with the owner or their
real estate agent, not the Owners Corporation. Properties in the building for sale or to lease can sometimes be
found on agent websites or on property listing portals.
